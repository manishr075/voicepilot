🎙️ Hands-Free Voice Commands

"Shazam, bold" Toggles bold styling on selected text or current paragraph.
"Shazam, italic" Toggles italic styling on selected text or current paragraph.
"Shazam, heading" Toggles between Heading 2 and regular Paragraph.
"Shazam, list" Creates or toggles a bullet point list item.
"Shazam, put a comma next to hello" Instant targeted AI edit directly applied to your document.
"Shazam, review" Full AI review for flow, tone, and grammar with in-place diff.
"Shazam, accept" / "Shazam, decline" Choose to keep or discard proposed AI edits.
"Shazam, undo" / "Shazam, redo" Revert or reapply previous actions locally.
"Shazam, read" / "Shazam, stop" Read draft aloud using browser text-to-speech or stop playback.
"Shazam, clear" Clears the document canvas (undoable via "Shazam, undo").
