Voice Command Reference — Hands-Free Voice Control
Shazam, <any instruction> Say ANY targeted edit (e.g. "Shazam, add a comma after hello" or "Shazam, make this friendlier"). Applies directly into your draft!
Shazam, review Comprehensive polish. Opens the two-screen comparison screen to review and accept/decline.
Shazam, accept / keep new Accepts the AI Review proposal into your draft. Also works with "option two" or "yes".
Shazam, decline / keep previous Declines the AI Review proposal and keeps your original text. Also works with "option one" or "no".
Shazam, undo Reverts the most recent insertion or accepted revision.
Shazam, redo Restores an undone action.
Shazam, read Reads your current draft aloud using high-quality speech synthesis.
Shazam, make that a separate note Moves your latest spoken phrase into the Separate Notes scratchpad.
