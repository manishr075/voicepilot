Voice Command Reference — Hands-Free Voice Control
Shazam, <any instruction> Say ANY targeted edit (e.g. "Shazam, put a comma next to hello"). Applied directly to your draft and saved to undo history.
Shazam, review Full comprehensive polish. Highlights proposed additions in green and removals in red on the field with the AI's reason.
Shazam, accept / keep new Accepts the proposed review changes into your document. Also works with "option two" or "yes".
Shazam, decline / keep previous Declines the proposed review changes and keeps your original text. Also works with "option one" or "no".
Shazam, clear Clears the draft document. Automatically saved to undo history so you can say "Shazam, undo" to restore anytime.
Shazam, undo Reverts the most recent insertion or accepted revision. Always works locally.
Shazam, redo Restores an undone action. Always works locally.
Shazam, read Reads your current draft aloud using speech synthesis. Always works locally.
Shazam, stop / silence Immediately halts speech synthesis playback if you want to silence reading.
Shazam, copy Copies your active draft directly to your clipboard hands-free.
Shazam, make that a separate note Moves your latest spoken phrase into the Separate Notes scratchpad.
