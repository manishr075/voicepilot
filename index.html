VoicePilot User Guide

⚡ How Shazam Works

VoicePilot is optimized for Wispr Flow and natural voice dictation. Speak your words directly into the document. Whenever you want to command the AI, speak your wake word: Shazam!

The Core Formula:
[Words behind Shazam] = Context (your draft)
[Words next to Shazam] = Instructions (what to do)

Example: "My flight leaves at 4pm Shazam change 4pm to 5:30pm" → VoicePilot uses your existing draft as context, applies the instruction, and immediately updates the text to "My flight leaves at 5:30pm".

🚀 Direct Voice Edits (Instant)

Direct voice edit commands apply instantly into your document without confirmation barriers:

  • Shazam, change 5pm to 6pm — Replaces words or phrases.
  • Shazam, make N capital in my name — Precise capitalization.
  • Shazam, put a comma next to hello — Targeted punctuation.
  • Shazam, turn this into 3 bullet points — Restructures layout.
  • Shazam, make it sound professional — Rewrites tone.

✨ AI Polish & Review (In-Place Highlights)

Say Shazam, polish or Shazam, review (or click the ✨ Polish button).

VoicePilot highlights improvements directly in your document:

Red = Replaced / Deleted Green = Corrected / Added

Say Shazam, keep to accept all improvements, or Shazam, discard (or Shazam, undo) to revert.

🎙️ Hands-Free Utility Commands

  • Shazam, undo / Shazam, redo — Step backward or forward in history.
  • Shazam, bold / italic / heading / list — Instant rich formatting.
  • Shazam, read / Shazam, stop — Read draft aloud or silence playback.
  • Shazam, copy — Copies entire document to clipboard.
  • Shazam, make that a separate note — Saves text into your side notes drawer.
